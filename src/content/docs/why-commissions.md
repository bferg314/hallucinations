---
title: "Why \"Commissions\"?"
---

The writing in this collection was produced by various AI models over the course of several years. I directed, prompted, and shaped each piece, sometimes loosely, sometimes with heavy involvement at every step, but I didn't write any of it in the traditional sense. The word "commissions" describes that relationship more closely. A commissioner provides the intent, the vision, and often detailed feedback throughout the process, while the execution belongs to someone (or something) else. It's a word that has always covered the full spectrum from "surprise me" to "I need exactly this," and it doesn't misrepresent anyone's contribution. These are things I caused to exist, not things I wrote.

It's also worth acknowledging what these models are. A massive body of human thought and expression got compressed into something I can have a working relationship with, and I pay for access to that relationship. That's the material the commission is made from: the gathered and embodied output of countless human minds. The ideas and direction are mine. The execution draws on all of humanity, and these commissions are something new in the pool. It's up to you to decide whether it's a Baby Ruth or something else... But I enjoy them, otherwise why would I share them?
